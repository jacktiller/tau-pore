Why it matters

7.4 million

Americans age 65 and older are living with Alzheimer’s (2026 estimate). That is about 1 in 9 people in that age group.

Source: Alzheimer’s Association, Facts and Figures ↗
1 in 3

older Americans dies with Alzheimer’s or another dementia.

Source: Alzheimer’s Association, Facts and Figures ↗
Tangles track symptoms

Tau builds up into tangles inside neurons. In autopsy studies, tangles line up with how bad memory and thinking problems were more closely than amyloid plaques do.

Source: Nelson, Braak & Markesbery, 2009 ↗
